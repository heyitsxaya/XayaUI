Decisions and why:
- Thresholds are scanned from the game's converter, not typed in, so they follow every patch and level. If the converter does not apply diminishing returns in game, the panel shows no thresholds and flags any disagreement with the game's current value (#325 open question).
- #290: its own record (2026-11-02-2358 entry) is about removing data copied from another addon. Xaya confirmed outside numbers from credible databases are allowed. No database numbers were added in this build: none could be checked from this session, so Spell Math takes a typed coefficient and shows it next to the measured ratio.
- Not given by the game, shown as such: primary to armor, primary to dodge, Strength to parry, stat to block, attack power to weapon damage, mastery's percent modifiers. Candidates for a named database source later.
- Estimates (labelled): unhasted attack speed and GCD = live x (1 + haste); haste rating for a target assumes haste sources multiply.

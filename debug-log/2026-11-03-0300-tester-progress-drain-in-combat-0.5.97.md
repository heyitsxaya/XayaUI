# Tester: Anti-Magic Shell still shows no duration after pulling 0.5.96 (XayaUI 0.5.97 / engine 0.4.2, fork: main)
Logged 2026-11-03 ~03:00 UTC. GitHub issue: #311. Tester Insivius (via Xaya): "still no duration with the new pull"; screenshot shows the art full and static in combat; full rule export attached (addonVersion 0.5.96).
Finding (from the full export): his display is not an icon with a timer. It is a texture cue ("AMS Progress", kind spellicon, 94 x 128, y -28, opacity 0.5, background on) with the PROGRESS DRAIN on (fill.enabled, direction bottom) and flash on; its time TEXT is OFF. So the "duration" he means is the drain. The engine (0.4.x) drew single slots as a plain icon with optional time text: no drain, no background, full opacity, the aura's own icon. Out of combat XayaUI draws the drain itself; in combat it cannot (the remaining fraction is secret), so the art stayed full.
Change: Blizzard's aura button can drive a StatusBar from the aura's duration (CustomAuraButton:SetDurationBar -> StatusBar:SetTimerDuration, in secure code, verified in Blizzard's UI source; options.direction = StatusBarTimerDirection RemainingTime / ElapsedTime). Single slots now: painted with the display's own art through XayaUI's art routine (new ns.PaintDisplayArt: kind, texture / atlas / spell icon, zoom, rotation, mirror, tint, desaturation), its background and opacity; a progress display becomes a duration bar with that art, orientation and reverse fill from XayaUI's direction (bottom / top / left / right, matching Visual.lua CropTex) and RemainingTime (or ElapsedTime when "reverse" is on). A slot whose look changes is rebuilt. /xuiengine no longer lists the drain as missing for single slots (still listed inside groups, whose buttons take each aura's own icon); flash and drain start / end limits stay listed.
Verified offline: aura_display_run includes his exact display (own slot 94 x 128, vertical bar emptying with remaining time, painted with his art, opacity 0.5, background, no text; edited direction rebuilds as horizontal reversed elapsed); all suites pass; whole-toc bench identical decisions.
Unverified in game: whether the status bar crops the art as it empties (expected, as StatusBar fills do) or stretches it; the drain in combat itself.

## 1. Diagnosis (reproduced before the fix)
In Auto size a group's container is a 1 x 1 frame (Visual.lua Container, `SetSize(max(1, cW or 1))`), and everything inside hangs from
its CENTRE: static displays (Place, their own anchor on the 1 x 1 box), a dynamic group's first item at `CENTER + DynamicFirstOffset`
(Right: (0, -h/2)), and the combat engine's container from the same frame. So "This group's anchor point = Bottom left" put the bottom-left
corner of an invisible dot on the frame's corner, and the grid sat half an icon left of it and a whole icon below its top (her case at
scale 0.25, 30 px icons: 3.625 px left, 7.375 px low on screen). Reproduced in tests/group_anchors_run.py and tests/layout_parity_run.py
on 897ae70: "grid 526.38,324.62 vs frame 530.00,332.00".

  screen with no frame named in Relative to, else 1 (Container's divisor). Derivation: before, the container's point P was on the
  target's point Q and the contents hung from C = Q - pv; now O's point is on Q', so O stays where it was when Q' = C + o. Her group:
  ((-15 + 0.5) x 0.25, (-30 + 0.5) x 0.25) = (-3.625, -7.375). The groups adjusted are saved in `CoreS.groupAnchorMoved` (id, name,
  point, grid / outline, dx, dy) for the walkthrough's B353 note; one chat line at that login says how many.
- The settings note (RulesTree.lua, Group Type & Container) now says: "Width / height give the container a fixed box, and the anchor
  point is that box's point. In Auto size the anchor point is that point of what the group holds: the whole grid of a dynamic group, or
  the outline of every switched-on display of a static group (also ones not showing now); Center, Centered row and Centered column keep

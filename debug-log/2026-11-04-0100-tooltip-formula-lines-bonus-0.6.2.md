# Spell tooltips show how cooldowns and damage come about, from live game values (Bonus 0.6.1 -> 0.6.2, fork: claude/exciting-hypatia-huaqbv)
Logged 2026-11-04 ~01:00 UTC. Request (Xaya): "hover over abilities and see the formulas for how cooldowns and damage get calculated" (screenshot: Fracture, 3.5 sec recharge, 2 charges, 15,386 Physical damage). Related roadmap items: #325 (spell info panel), #339 (developer plugin).
Decisions (Xaya, this session): #290 applies only to information officially marked as an addon's or another third party's, or obtained against Blizzard's terms (ROADMAP standing rule reworded); learning directly from the game at runtime is strongly preferred; later, a capture walkthrough in the developer plugin can observe numbers across gear changes per spec and talents, and the fitted results may ship as per-class load-on-demand data addons.
Change: TT.FormulaLines (Tooltips.lua 1b), spell tooltips only, out of combat only, own master switch (independent of the ID lines). Recharge / Cooldown: GetSpellCharges cooldownDuration / chargeModRate (2+ charges) or GetSpellCooldown duration / modRate (while running, GCD ignored); "(haste N%)" when the rate equals 1 + UnitSpellHaste / 100 within 0.01, else "(rate)"; compared with the time the tooltip prints ("= tooltip" within 0.06 s, otherwise "tooltip says"); base from GetSpellBaseCooldown; idle spells show the base only. Damage ratio: each "N <school> damage" in GetSpellDescription (English clients) / attack power (UnitAttackPower total) or GetSpellBonusDamage(school) when intellect is the highest primary stat; "over N sec" kept with its own number. TT.FormulaSource: optional hook for learned data (nil today). New Formulas tab (master, cooldown, damage).
Unverified in game (assumptions the "= tooltip" check is meant to expose): that cooldownDuration / duration are the unhasted times and chargeModRate / modRate carry haste (Blizzard's cooldown frames take modRate separately; not confirmed for 12.1.5); that cooldownDuration is reported while charges are full; that UnitStat's second return is the effective stat.
Checks: tests/tooltip_formulas_run.py 19/19 (recharge = tooltip, mismatch reported, non-haste rate, base vs running, idle base, GCD ignored, two damage numbers with over-time, spell power for intellect, secret stat skipped, combat, non-English, options, items, throwing API, hook, tab); the parser bug that tied "over 9 sec" to the wrong number was caught by these checks and fixed. tests/tooltips_run.py 47/47.

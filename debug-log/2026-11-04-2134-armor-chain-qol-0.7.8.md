## Where the number comes from (V2)
- The game has no call that returns "armor per point of Agility". For Vengeance the link is the mastery, whose text the game fills
  in: "Increases Armor by 88.7% of your Agility" (her screenshot). The toolkit already reads that text (`MasterySpells` +
  `C_Spell.GetSpellDescription`, `namespaces.md:4402`: MayReturnNothing, SecretArguments=AllowedWhenTainted).
- `CM.ArmorPerPrimary(ctx)` matches `<STAT_ARMOR> by N% of your <SPELL_STATn_NAME>` in the mastery text (lower case, colour codes
  removed, a decimal comma read) and returns N. No percent or formula is typed in. A text with no such words, or naming another
  stat than the primary one, gives nil and the old "not given by the game" line.
- INFERENCES, to check in game (QA E1u): that the mastery adds armor linearly from total Agility (the text's wording); which
  UnitArmor figure holds it (base or the buffed part); that armor buffs which multiply armor are not in the change (said in the
  hover); other specs' wording (only matched if their mastery text uses the same pattern).
- Damage reduction through `C_PaperDollInfo.GetArmorEffectiveness(armor, level)` as before (new local `ArmorDRChange`).
